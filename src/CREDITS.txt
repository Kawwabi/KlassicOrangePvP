Thanks to xokz and MiffenKop, creators of the Connected Textures Overhaul, which I adapted to 1.8.9 and added the texture to my own.
Thanks also to Screaming Brain Studios for making the cubemap that I edited and adapted for Minecraft.
The other textures in this pack are my own creations or from Minecraft 26.2 itself, although most of them are slightly edited. If you're the curious type who wants to know how I changed the saturation of things, it's a little Linux command that I ran in a few folders.

Here is the command:

find . -type f -iname "*.png" -exec mogrify -modulate 100,165 -brightness-contrast -2x2 {} +
